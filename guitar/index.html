How this works
  1. Warm-up 5: start the 30-minute timer, then Breakfast of Champions.
  2. Fretboard 5: odd days Notes in 4ths, even days two Shape Bank items, through the keys in 4ths.
  3. Focus 10: tap Today, then Start, and follow Today's order. My book's day page says why.
  4. Play 10: my player of the week, below. I learn the song by ear, then check it in Songsterr.
  5. Done: tap Mark this day done. Today then opens the next day.
  6. Song buttons: Songsterr is the standard tab, My Version is my own Songsterr tab, Lesson opens the song's folder in my Google Drive Lessons.
  7. Class: I play and mark up my class pages in forScore. My class songs, below, holds my cues and links for each one.
  8. Always the icon: I open this app from my Fretboard Home Screen icon, not a Safari tab, so my progress stays in one place and it works offline.
Offline. My app works as usual. For songs I use the Songsterr app (songs I've opened before), forScore, and my Drive offline copies.

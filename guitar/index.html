How this works
  1. Warm-up 5: start the 30-minute timer, then Breakfast of Champions.
  2. Fretboard 5: odd days Notes in 4ths, even days two Shape Bank items, through the keys in 4ths.
  3. Focus 10: tap Today, then Start, and follow Today's order. My book's day page says why.
  4. Play 10: On my stand, below the fretboard: my class pieces, my lesson and my own. I play the one that needs me today, then mark it Clean, Getting there or Messy.
  5. Done: at the end of my 30 minutes, Mark this day done, at the bottom. Today then opens the next day.
  6. Open: one button for each piece. It opens my own copy in forScore when I have one, otherwise My Version, otherwise Songsterr. Video, when there is one, is the lesson.
  7. My music: my Library and my Ideas shelf sit below my stand. I search or filter, tap Open to play, or + Stand to put a piece on my stand in Class, Lesson or My own. A fretboard drill goes on my stand the same way, from + Stand under Today's order. Music I've loaded into forScore myself goes on with + Add my own piece. My master copies live in my Library folder in iCloud and Drive.
  8. Class: I play and mark up my class pages in forScore. My stand holds my class pieces in handout order, with the next class's marked.
  9. Always the icon: I open this app from my Fretboard Home Screen icon, not a Safari tab, so my progress stays in one place and it works offline.
Offline. My app works as usual. forScore buttons open my own copies; Songsterr buttons open the Songsterr app, where songs I've opened before still play. Class pages are in forScore.
